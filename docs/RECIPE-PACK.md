# Exploration pack v1 — authoring and review notes

The ten optional recipes in `examples/exploration-pack.json` are original cooking drafts for testing discovery and variation workflows. They are not kitchen-tested; estimates are not guarantees. Dinner profiles are authored for three portions and breakfast profiles for one. Additional portion profiles must be written and reviewed explicitly.

Suggested review pairs:

| Starting recipe | Possible variation | Axis |
|---|---|---|
| EXP-D01 Lemon–tahini tofu & broccoli roast | EXP-D02 Smoky–lime tofu & broccoli roast | Flavour |
| EXP-D01 | EXP-D03 Lemon–tahini chickpea & broccoli roast | Main ingredient |
| EXP-D01 | EXP-D04 Pan-seared lemon–tahini tofu & broccoli | Technique |
| EXP-B01 Apple–cinnamon yoghurt breakfast | EXP-B02 Cocoa–banana yoghurt breakfast or EXP-B03 Berry–vanilla yoghurt breakfast | Flavour |
| EXP-B04 Smoky–lime eggs on toast | EXP-B05 Smoky–lime egg & spinach wrap | Breakfast format |
| EXP-B04 | EXP-B06 Mustard–herb eggs on toast | Flavour |

These suggestions are not pre-approved links. Review complete instructions and quantities through Manage variations. Selecting a different flavour may also change vegetables, dressings, utensils and timing; the comparison shows those changes.

Recorded ingredient compositions are illustrative minimum definitions, not product-specific allergen guarantees. Check packaging for bread, tortillas, yoghurt, mustard, nut butter and other compound ingredients before relying on exclusions. No recipe is labelled gas-free, low-FODMAP or medically appropriate.

Food-safety reference pages checked during authoring:
- Health Canada, Safe cooking temperatures: https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/general-food-safety-tips/safe-internal-cooking-temperatures.html
- Health Canada, Food safety tips for leftovers: https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/general-food-safety-tips/food-safety-tips-leftovers.html

The egg instructions use Health Canada's 74°C egg-dish endpoint. Storage notes distinguish cooked components from cold toppings and follow the general 2–3-day refrigerated-leftover window. These references inform handling notes; they do not constitute endorsement or testing of the recipes.
